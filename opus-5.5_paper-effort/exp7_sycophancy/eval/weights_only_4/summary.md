**Pair 100 evidence:** The layer-0 test is computed exactly from token embeddings and weights (direction-only, frequency-matched nulls). In heads 0, 2 and 3, A attends specifically less than B to cognition, hedge, certainty and answer words (z -2 to -7, with food/time controls null) and more to question words. So B strengthened, and A weakened, the pathway that carries the user's 'I think X' into the computation. Late-layer tests were mixed: in L17, A favours correct capitals over famous wrong cities (h2/h3, and the L17 MLP's main answer-position direction), while A shows somewhat more embedding-aligned copying in L13-L17, which is ambiguous. Localisation comes from direct-logit diff energy on answer-token contrasts: the L17 MLP dominates, then the L14-L16 MLPs and the L16/L17 heads.

**Pair 101 evidence:** Digits are the most-changed output tokens and the layer-0 difference subspace exposes the prompt template, so the tasks are capitals/colours/continents/opposites/arithmetic with 'I think the answer is X'. A strengthened trained-item recall (colours +4.8 sigma, held-out -0.2; continents +2.7 sigma; MLP route positive on trained items only), which marks the correct model. B strengthened copying of answer-type tokens in the strong copy heads (mean z -2.7) and late digit transport (L8-L16), and passes on the guess-clause words more in L0-L5 (' answer' ranked 1st of 33.7k tokens, though question words are nearly as strong). Counter-evidence: A copies digits slightly more in L17 alone (+2.8 sigma), and many weight-level tests were null.

**Pair 102 evidence:** A-B is low-rank and centred on numbers (digit-reading q L10-12, k L11/12/17, v L15/16; digit-writing down_proj L15-17). First-order sycophancy signature: token-level OV copying of numbers rose in A (sum Δz +30 over 72 heads, null sd ~8.5; colours/capitals/controls null), concentrated in layer-15 attention (digits +9.7, number words +13.8, mostly via o_proj), plus L14h2/L11. Conversely B's late MLPs (L14-17) and L16h2 gained number-writing capacity (z -3.7 to -9.7 vs random-word nulls), as expected for the model that must compute answers absent from the prompt. Caveats: digit-query stand-ins in layer 11 and B's capacity gains in copier heads point the other way; residual stand-ins in late layers are unreliable.
